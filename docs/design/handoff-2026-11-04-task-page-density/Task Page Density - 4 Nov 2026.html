Task page density · 4 Nov 2026
Five paired frames, each the task page or a seated card as built on 4 Nov (left) beside the same surface with the forensic layer demoted (right). Nothing is removed from the app: every fact, id and label on the left is still reachable on the right, one disclosure or one switch away. The vocabulary, ladder and grounds are unchanged; what changes is which altitude each thing draws at by default.
What this page is
A proposal for the task page (TaskPageView) and the cards it seats (DecisionCardComposition). The left frames are redrawn from the 4 Nov as-built page and the 8 Oct refined cards; the right frames apply three rules, below. Each ◆ is a decision the owner has to make; the rest is drawing.
The three rules
1 · Three altitudes. Glance (title, one status line, hold, the ask and its actions); read (the body, the current run and round); forensic (ids, digests, admission facts, hold codes, the event log). 2 · One affordance per altitude. The forensic layer is one closed Details fold at the foot of a card or section, never five differently named folds, plus one app-wide switch that opens all of them. 3 · Say each fact once, at the altitude it is decided at. A hash the operator never decides on is never in the reading path.
What the rules do not touch
Plan §9's labeling rule (agent-written text is visibly a claim) holds on every frame: each quoted block keeps its dashed frame and the card keeps the word (unverified) once, at the top. R6 (one filled control, only with a recommendation), the consequence sheet, the R9 fact row, both ladders and the outline row are unchanged. Frames are Mac, day; the phone takes the same order on its ladder.

2 · One switch opens every Details at once
The cases that need the forensic layer are debugging cases, and in those you want all of it on every card and section, not one fold at a time. A View menu item (⌥⌘D) and the same toggle in the iPhone's More Actions open every Details fold on the page and keep them open on this device until switched off. It replaces nothing; the folds still open one at a time by hand.
Proposed · View menu, Mac
Show Sidebar⌃⌘S
Refresh⌘R
Show Technical Detail⌥⌘D
Show Exact Times⌥⌘T
Checked, every Details fold on the current page is open and fact rows carry their exact value (full digest, ISO instant) in place of the short form. Unchecked, the folds return to the per-device state they had. Show Exact Times is the same idea for the hover-and-long-press time pattern the app already uses; it is listed here as a sibling, not proposed.
