Unified list and task page · 30 Oct 2026
Fold the Inbox and Tasks views into one list and one task page on both clients, and remove the Mac inspector everywhere. This is a unification, not a rewrite: the model, the daemon, the cards, the rows, the sheets, the faces, the ladders and the two grounds decided on 30 Oct (Overcast by day, Dead Channel by dusk) are untouched. Band 1 is the baseline as built; bands 2–4 the Mac list, the Mac task page and iPhone; band 5 one accessibility size; band 6 the new atoms in both grounds. Each caption reads platform · surface · rules applied · ◆ count.
What changes
Presentation only, both clients. The Inbox and Tasks views fold into one list (Needs you · Working · Finished, newest first) and one task page. The Mac loses its inspector on every surface and goes from three columns to two; Claims, Evidence and Technical Bindings fold into the card’s foot as they already do on iPhone. The Resolved scope goes away (resolved items are on the timeline; closed scoped items row in Finished). Urgency leaves the UI. Devices moves into a Settings sheet. One new fixed element: the outline row. Eight new atoms — a section head, the outline row, a page header, a one-line item row, the Task fold, the sidebar tools row, the project filter popover and the Settings sheet — every one composed from existing recipes (R1, R2, R3, R9, R11).
What does not change
The design language as decided on 30 Oct: Overcast by day and Dead Channel by dusk, every token in that handoff’s tokens.css, untouched. The three faces and both ladders (R10). All 14 decision cards, their module orderings, action hierarchy, consequence sheets and claim labelling — the card below is the card as built. The row vocabulary (R31), state chips (R4), keyword heads (R1), sentence disclosures (R2), quotes (R5), notices (R14), the empty state (R13), fact rows (R9), the rail (R15). Every sheet. The standing notices. The system-chrome list. No daemon, API, contract or command change; the client already joins items to tasks by task id.
Decided 30 Oct
Urgency is out of the UI: no chip, light, count or sort key in the list, no priority chip on any card eyebrow, no wax chip in the menu-bar panel; the wire field stays. Sort: newest open item first; the newest card leads a multi-item task. Counts are rows, everywhere. Devices → Settings, from the toolbar slot it held. Readers open as a sheet on the Mac. iPhone: one list, no tab bar, the tools row under the title, Settings trailing. Inspector: removed on every surface; its content folds into the card foot.
◆ Six owner decisions
Each frame’s caption counts the ◆ it draws a default for; the list under the frame names them. 1 multi-item row · 2 snoozed proposal · 3 pinning · 4 naming · 5 outline trailing slot · 6 items without a task. Above them all, daemon-side: should a task hold more than one open item? If not, ◆1, the Decisions menu and the one-line rows are not built. The README carries the gate.
