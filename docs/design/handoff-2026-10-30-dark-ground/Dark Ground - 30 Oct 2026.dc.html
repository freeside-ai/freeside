GoalThe only candidate that changes what the accent is. On umber, tawny was a lighter brown; on neutral graphite it becomes metal against stone — the signet mark, the Needs-decision chip and the one filled button carry the whole room. That is the villa: a cold house with one lit object. Nothing else had to get louder.
AccentHolds. accentText 7.34:1 on ground2 (was 6.18 on Straylight), and the filled button’s ground2 label is 7.34:1. Still gold-ochre: the hue did not move, only its surroundings.
Wax · waterBoth gain. Wax 5.38:1, and because the ground is no longer red-brown, the Unverified notice is unmistakably a different colour from the card. Water 7.30:1 and reads as teal rather than a cooler dim ink, so links finally look like links.
ElevationBest ladder of the three: ground→ground2 1.08, ground2→ground3 1.12, ground→sidebar 1.08. Weak spots as given: itemBorder (#43474D) at 1.8:1 on ground2 is the card’s only edge and nearly disappears; milestonePrior rings at 1.8:1 next to a 1.7:1 connector; waxWash reads as mud. All tuned below.
RiskThe given greys lean blue (ruleStrong #7B828A, inkFaint #7E8187: blue 15 above red) while ink and dim ink are warm. Two temperatures on one surface reads as two palettes and drifts toward the rejected Nocturne. The tuned set pulls those three greys to within 6 of neutral; the ink stays warm, as it should — it is the vellum, dimmed.

GoalNear-black makes the accent loudest of all (8.63:1) and ink the brightest (15.84:1). It is the most “powerful” first impression — and the one that most resembles a terminal theme, which the invariants rule out. On an iPhone OLED, #0A0908 is effectively off, and a warm-black field with gold text and a hairline grid is the cyberpunk costume by other means.
AccentHolds on contrast, fails on register: #DBA63A on near-black is the brightest, most saturated-looking tawny of the four, and the step to “yellow” is short. The filled button starts to glow without any glow being drawn.
Wax · waterBoth fine numerically (wax 6.21, water 8.40). But every wash is within 1.1:1 of its ground, so a notice is a border with nothing inside it, and a diff’s add/remove lines are coloured text on nothing.
Elevationground→ground2 1.05, ground2→ground3 1.07, ground→sidebar 1.02. The ladder is gone by design: cards exist only as hairlines, and the given rule (#262321) is 1.2:1, so they barely exist. Sidebar at 1.02:1 from ground is invisible. To work, rule would have to come up to ~3:1 everywhere, which turns the villa into a wireframe.
VerdictThe most exciting swatch and the least Freeside surface. Long reading at 16:1 also halates on a Mac at night.
Recommendation · Dead Channel, tunedmid-tones and washes first, then the Increased Contrast cuts · day values unchanged

Dead Channel. It is the only ground that changes the accent’s meaning instead of its volume, and it does so without touching the accent, the type, the controls or the mark. The tuning below is small and mostly about the middle of the ladder, which the candidate table had not addressed.

What changed. ground3 lifted one step so the selected row and the quote block read on their own; itemBorder separated from milestonePrior and lifted so the card has an edge; secondaryBorder and milestonePrior lifted for the same reason; the three structural greys (ruleStrong, inkFaint, secondaryBorder) pulled toward neutral so the chrome and the warm ink stop reading as two palettes; every wash lifted a step because on a neutral ground a tinted wash needs less lightness to read as colour, and the given values read as dirt. Accent, wax, water, ink, dim ink and the diff colours are as given.

What fails and the fix. rule, secondaryBorder and itemBorder sit below 3:1 on every ground — as they do in Straylight, Soot and Lacquer, and by design: they are tints of the ground, and separation is carried by the ground steps. That is exactly why they promote to ruleStrong (4.47:1 on ground2) under Increased Contrast; the promotion is the fix and it is already in DesignLanguage.swift. ruleStrong, accentBorder and inkFaint clear 3:1 on ground, ground2 and ground3. Every text token clears 4.5:1 on ground and ground2; the two closest are waxText on ground3 (4.67:1, a wax-outlined control on hover) and inkFaint on ground3 (3.84:1, disabled, floor 3:1). Nothing is listed red in the table.
