Interfaces, 6 Oct 2026 — what changed on main since 18 Sept, drawn from the SwiftUI source and the visual-audit handoff of 2 Oct (decisions D01–D09, all Keep on Mac and iPhone). The owner chose a focused hierarchy pass over the 1 Oct redesign: louder decisions, quieter supporting detail, same tokens, same keyword headings. Four cards were recomposed (question, final review, dispute, findings); the inbox row, task hold, long messages and empty forms were refined; and three standing surfaces landed outside the audit — the Stopped indicator (#980), the follow-up filing card (#1633) and View PR on a publish hold (#1643). Day frames are Mac; dusk frames are iPhone.
Assessment · main as of 2026-10-06
Eighteen days: the decision gets louder, everything else gets quieter

Four rounds of native Mac/iPhone comparisons settled it: no redesign, no new type scale, no serif headings. Instead each of the four decision-first cards now puts the thing to decide — the question, the verdict, the claim, the finding — in the first viewport, and folds the recorded context, run coordinates and source identifiers one disclosure away. Nothing was removed; the handoff's "information moved out of the initial view" table names where each fact went.

Landed · the audit (plan rev. 78)
  • D01 Inbox rows — summary in the serif at regular weight, type name and badges on a quiet line above, project and time below. Count sits only beside Inbox.
  • D02 Task hold — a callout under the chip in task detail: round over Hold: … on the accent wash behind a leading bar.
  • D03 Unverified — the label stays; its explanation opens from an ⓘ by pointer, keyboard, touch and VoiceOver.
  • D04 Long messages — six lines then Read full message; expansion is in place, per message, never saved.
  • D05 New task — keyword labels outside the fields, muted placeholders that are never submitted.
  • D06 Agent question — the question in the serif, what it blocks in dim, each option a filled panel with a leading rule; no generic ask above.
  • D07 Final review — readiness checklist first (exceptions lead, passed rows fold), unboxed summary, then View PR as the one filled button.
  • D08 Dispute — reason as the ask's dim second line, the claim as prose under Agent claims, metadata folded.
  • D09 Findings — one card per finding: exact message, proposed route, producer label, its own Reason and alternatives. Accept all dispositions says what it covers.
Landed · outside the audit
  • Stopped (#980) — a wax row under the freshness banner and a card plus status-item dot in the menu bar, driven by the sync snapshot, never inferred from items. A cached stop says so.
  • Follow-up filing (#1633) — the second effect_proposal variant: repository, labels, milestone, source finding, text screening as facts; title and body in full as unverified text.
  • Publish blocked (#1643) offers View PR when the hold carries one; Revoked banner gains Pair Again (#1458); Mismatch banner and menu card show both contract digests (#1265).
  • Review rounds — verdict rows with Round facts folded; shortened revisions are eight characters everywhere.
Not adopted

From the 1 Oct proposal: the global register drop (faint 10.5pt facts everywhere), serif section headings, flat hairline rows, retiring the keywords, and the single filled action per surface. The accepted principle is narrower — reduce repeated labels and framing before reducing type — and it applies only to the reviewed surfaces.

Left behind on main

The composition switches are exhaustive by type, so the ten unaudited cards (spec approval, execution failure, diminishing returns, contradiction, configuration, publish blocked, task and effect proposals, system health, blocked) still draw the boxed CONTEXT label, the dashed agent frame and the repeated unverified sentence. The survey treats that as the first thing to unify; its recommended grammar (4b) is drawn on this canvas beside the as-built final-review card.
