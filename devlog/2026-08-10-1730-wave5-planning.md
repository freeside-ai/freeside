## Decisions

**A new vocabulary contract core (#652) heads the wave's chain instead
of a rescoped #22.** The wave's lanes (elaborator #655, intake #659,
disposition publication #525) need new domain types: elaboration-stage
vocabulary, research/spec artifact kinds, per-finding disposition
persistence (§7's prerequisite for #525, revision 28), and initiator
configuration. #22 was considered and rejected as the carrier: its
recorded scope is member-widening of four specific provisional enums
(Priority, ItemStatus, SensitivityClass, Author), and rescoping it into
a general new-type unit would erase that standing condition record. #22
stays dormant and delegates any wave-5 enum-member need to #652 under
its own acceptance rules.

**The chain runs #652 → #653 → #654 → #657, behind the in-flight #557
(PR #646).** The §11 row fixes only the algebra-then-retrofit relative
order; revision 26 keeps both off the loop's critical path. Putting the
vocabulary core first unblocks three lanes at the earliest slot; the
runs list/timeline (#657) sits last because it is depth, not
substrate, and is one cross-component unit (api + daemon sync + app)
per the monorepo rule rather than a spec PR with trailing consumers.
Rejected alternative: folding each lane's vocabulary need into its
feature unit as a kind:contract unit, which would serialize whole
features through the exclusive chain (the wave-4 #479 pattern argued
against exactly this).

**Intake (#659) sequences behind the run_proposal retrofit (#654).**
Building intake against the pre-registry admission surface and adapting
it after the retrofit would pay the same adaptation twice; the
idempotency-key contract the retrofit lands is precisely what makes
label re-observation safe.

**Staging beyond the row's named work.** The row names ten of the
wave's eighteen units. Of the other eight, one is #652, created in
this session (above); the sweep staged the remaining seven on their
recorded wave-4 re-examination conditions or live operational need:
#448/#451 (the production Codex
token expires 2026-08-13; sustained unattended review needs the
refresh/re-enrollment pair), #381 (#525 makes its durable claim
surface real), #470 (real-backlog implementation ergonomics), #492/#493
(live review-path fixes, lane-assigned ward: both failure sites live in
ward's Codex review lifecycle), #650 (live infinite re-poll on the
completion path; conservative shape only, the acknowledgment question
stays the owner's). Rejected alternative: deferring all seven to the
wave-7 drain, which would leave the production review path running on
a credential with a three-day fuse.

**Width is accepted, flagged, and shaped.** Eighteen units exceeds any
prior wave; ten are the row's own work. Mitigation is structural: lane
heads carry the wave, the small fixes are fill-in behind them, and the
chain keeps contract exposure serialized.
