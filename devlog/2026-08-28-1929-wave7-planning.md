# Wave 7 Planning: Decision-Surface Chain Positions and Rescopes (#1001)

**Work unit:** the spine's Wave 7 planning session (tracker #1001), run
against plan revision 42 with revisions 40 and 41 binding. **Origin:** the
§11 wave-7 row leaves the contract-chain positions, the #961 placement,
and the producer and client-adoption units to planning, and two units it
names (#918, #920) described work the daemon already had.

## Decisions

1. **#942 heads the chain, ahead of #917.** The row lists #917 first, but
   #917's body carried the own-artifact-subtraction epoch that revision 40
   records as the rejected second inline attempt, and revision 40 says #942
   specifies the mechanism. A carrier cannot land before the thing it
   carries is defined, so #942 (plan §4 mechanism text, daemon persistence,
   the break tests) goes first and #917 `starts-after` it. Rejected: keeping
   the row order and letting #917 define the epoch itself, which is the
   inline path that failed three review rounds on PR #916.

2. **#917 and #724 are two consecutive chain links, not one unit.**
   Revision 40 says the §4 recommendation shape and the §9 typed minimum
   card facts land "as one serialized contract unit before producers and
   client adoption." Both #917 and #724 existed with overlapping scope, and
   the combined unit touches every size amplifier in
   `docs/coordination.md` (sync-carried fields, goldens, migration, mock
   state). Chose two links in order (#917 the recommendation shape and the
   `adjudicate` disposition; #724 the per-type facts plus the display
   names moved from #917), which keeps revision 40's intent: both merge
   before #1002, #1003, and #1004 start. Rejected: merging #724 into #917
   (one review of roughly 2,000 lines, the size the budget note shows
   multiplies convergence rounds). This is a spine scheduling choice
   under the unit-size budget, not an owner decision, and it is surfaced
   on #1001 as an open owner item. It does not overturn revision 40's
   intent: the §11 row that revision 41 added already lists #917 and
   #724 as separate chain positions, and before this planning both
   issues described the same §9 per-type fact fields, so the split
   resolves a duplication and both links still merge before any
   producer or client-adoption unit starts. The owner confirms or vetoes
   by fiat; a confirmation earns a supersession entry in
   docs/history/decisions.md as its own gated unit, and a veto merges
   #724 back into #917 before #917 is claimed.

3. **#918 and #920 are rescoped to the verified gap, not closed.** #943
   established that `applyDiscuss` and the spec-revision re-invocation
   exist. At `7916aae9`: the §5.14 permanent sync tests 5, 6, 7, and 12
   are green; `finding_adjudication` discuss supersedes through #948 and
   #843; `enqueueSpecRevision` re-runs elaboration. What is missing is the
   discuss consumers for `spec_approval` (folded from #710),
   `execution_failure`, `review_configuration`, and `review_dispute` if it
   exists (#918), and the revision facts on a revised `spec_approval`
   (#920). Both keep `kind:contract` until their planning stage confirms
   whether a shared-package change remains; #918 is explicitly relabeled
   off the chain if none does. Rejected: closing both as done (the row and
   the exit proof still need the consumers and the card facts) and keeping
   the stale bodies (which would have re-planned merged work).

4. **Client adoption is #693 rescoped, plus #1004.** The row names client
   adoption without an issue. #693 (the Mac-only spec-revision loop) was
   the existing issue for the same gap, so it became the discuss and
   request-changes convergence unit for both platforms, including the
   conversation surface `app/SURFACES.md` marks "Not yet." The
   recommendation-led rendering, per-type leads, and truthful capability
   filter are a separate saddle unit (#1004) because they wait on #917 and
   #724 while #693 is startable now. Each transaction contract unit ships
   its own client form, so no third adoption unit exists.

5. **Producers are two spine units.** #1002 wires the adjudicator's parked
   batch as the first `agent_judgment` recommendation (the §4 type case;
   no `daemon_policy` or `project_policy` producer is specified for Phase
   1, so none is planned). #1003 populates the §9 facts at every creation
   site behind #724 and #901. Rejected: one producer unit (two unrelated
   engine surfaces) and folding producers into the contract units (#917
   and #724 exclude them by their own non-goals).

6. **#419 joins the wave; #936 `starts-after` it.** §4 gives
   `publish_blocked` four actions; the daemon concludes
   `rerun_trust_evaluation` but the item does not offer it because no
   durable reevaluation consumer exists. #936's alternate-profile
   transaction re-runs trust evaluation on the same intent, so #419 lands
   first as non-contract engine work beside the chain. Rejected: leaving
   `rerun_trust_evaluation` unoffered (the 1B.1 phone-decidability claim
   covers every Phase 1 card action except `convert_to_policy`).

7. **#986 and #990 land in this wave, at chain positions 13 and 14.** No
   row names either. #990 produces `agent_question`, without which #919's
   answer transactions cannot be proven on real items; the owner decided
   #986 on 2026-08-28 and #990 `starts-after` it. Placed after the row's
   own units and before #924 so the slip order stays #924 first, then the
   pair. Rejected: deferring both to wave 8 (leaves the `agent_question`

8. **#961 goes to wave 9.** The row allows either. Its subject is the
   adjudicator inference boundary, which sits with the agent-vocabulary

9. **Chain length 15 is accepted, with a recorded slip order.** The
   wave-seven-split note says to split wave 7 if planning finds the chain

   13 closed in nine days. Chose to run the wave as planned: the slip
   order (#924, then #986 with #990) removes three links without a plan
   revision, while a realized split needs one. The decision point is the
   first chain stall, recorded on the tracker.

10. **The deferral drain is exactly the row's clusters.** 130 queue items

## Verification Findings That Changed the Work

- The Swift `ActionOutcome` still classifies `discuss` and `request_changes`
  as `.pending` and MockServer reuses it, so client convergence is real work
  even though the daemon side is done (decision 4).
- `api/openapi.yaml` carries none of the wave's sync fields yet: no
  `recommendation`, no decision-surface identity, no evidence media type or
  size, no pairing host identity or expiry, no telemetry endpoint. Every
  chain unit that introduces one of those fields is genuinely contract
  work; #918 and #920 hold `kind:contract` only until their planning
  stage confirms a shared-package change (decision 3).
- Only #578 and the wave tracker are pinned; `docs/coordination.md` still
  names #799 as a standing pin. Not a resolver defect; noted on #1001.

## Revisit When

- The chain stalls (an open contract PR older than the wave-6 median
  review cycle with links queued behind it): apply the slip order, then
  decide the split.
- A `daemon_policy` or `project_policy` recommendation producer is wanted:
  that is a plan §4 matter first, then a producer unit like #1002.
- #918's planning stage finds no shared-package change: relabel it off the
  chain and let the remaining links close up.
