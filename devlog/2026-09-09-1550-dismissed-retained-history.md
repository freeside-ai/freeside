# Dismissed Publication History Can Restore Run Access

Chose to let the retained verifier recognize a dismissed current ready card
as authenticated publication history. Requiring unfinished feedback for that
card conflated restoring the run's endpoint with authorizing another attempt.
Dismissal ends the card's action surface; it does not erase its publication.

The owner assigned this narrow lifecycle repair after a dismissed ready card
prevented the supported retained-session resume. The alternative of reopening
the card or replaying work would overwrite a valid decision or manufacture new
authority. Both remain prohibited.

The ordinary final verifier still requires an open ready item. Retained
verification still authenticates the publication binding, producer, export and
remote PR, and still refuses recorded work-unit completion. Superseded cards
continue to require the existing authenticated feedback or successor chain.
Only the current dismissed card gains the historical path; stopped and other
non-open states do not inherit it. The shared store and client authorization
rules are unchanged.

Refutation covers preserving the dismissed card and command through read-only
reopen, refusal of feedback on that card, refusal of final ready acceptance,
and rejection of missing or mismatched publication evidence. These checks use
real production fixtures and supported client commands; they do not establish
live restoration after installation.

Independent refutation confirmed that the existing negative checkpoint helper
could count its own state-mismatch error as a verifier rejection. The helper
now compares states only for expected success, so unexpected acceptance fails
the negative cases. The review found no remaining authorization defect after
that correction.

Revisit when the supported harness needs to serve history after a PR closes or
a work unit completes. That requires a separate lifecycle decision rather than
loosening the existing remote or completion gates in this repair.
