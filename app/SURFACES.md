# Client Surfaces

What the Freeside app (Mac and iOS) has to show, and how far along each piece
is. The plan (`docs/plan.md`) defines every surface; this file only tracks
status. Design decisions, layout, and wording are deliberately absent: the
**Open Questions** section is the agenda for a later UX pass.

**Keeping it current:** when a PR adds or changes something the app shows,
update its line here in the same PR. A contract unit that could answer an
open question either answers it or says it is leaving it open.

Status words: **Done** (built, both platforms), **Generic** (shows up in the
unified list and decision detail with the standard layout, nothing specific to it yet),
**Not yet** (defined by the plan or API, not built), **Later** (a later
phase), **Open** (the plan implies it but doesn't say where it goes).
Mac and iOS match on every line unless a line says otherwise.

## At a Glance

- **Done:** first-launch daemon address entry (no implicit sample data), pairing,
  the unified list, decision detail, task page, run timeline,
  freshness banner, Settings (the Mac window and the iPhone list) with Devices, the routing policy, and account usage in it, Mac menu bar, the sync
  and command-retry machinery, and the task-proposal card with its
  "start with changes" and snooze sheets (its full-artifact view is not),
  plus the shared ask-first decision shell, the contract recommendation
  rendered in its source register, action hierarchy, per-type typed card
  facts, the per-finding finding-adjudication card and its
  route choice list, plus the text-only conversation thread and its composer
  sheets on the refined vocabulary (R5, R11),
  and the New Task composer on the Mac list's tools row (⌘N) and the iPhone's bottom bar. Since the 8 Oct 2026
  pass the two clients draw that vocabulary on two type ladders
  (`FreesideLadder`): the Mac one runs ask 20, statement 15, label and body
  13, mono 12, keyword and chip 11, a 28pt button, and gaps of 18, 9, and 8;
  the iPhone one runs ask 24, statement 16, label 15, body and mono 13.5,
  keyword and chip 11.5, a 44pt button, and gaps of 20, 10, and 10.
- **Generic:** seven of the fourteen card types use only the shared decision
  shell; five additional types compose card-specific orderings from the shared
  graphic module set.
- **Not yet:** conversation attachments, the evidence
  packet viewer, proposal batches, usage beside the agent choices, the
  initiative view, push notifications.
- **Open:** seven placement questions, listed at the end.
- **Layout:** one list and one task page on both clients (decision page:
  [Unified List](../docs/design/handoff-2026-10-30-unified-list/Unified%20List%20-%2030%20Oct%202026.dc.html)).
- **Grounds:** Overcast by day and Dead Channel by dusk (decision pages:
  [Overcast](../docs/design/handoff-2026-10-30-dark-ground/Light%20Ground%20-%2030%20Oct%202026.dc.html),
  [Dead Channel](../docs/design/handoff-2026-10-30-dark-ground/Dark%20Ground%20-%2030%20Oct%202026.dc.html)).

## Open Questions

The plan implies each of these but doesn't say where it lives. They stay
open until a unit answers them; answering is a design decision.

1. **Editing a proposal before approving.** A sheet on `task_proposal` and on `effect_proposal` (one control, whether the PR closes the issue). Is that also right for batches?
2. **Where project- and system-level items go.** Answered by ◆6 of the [30 Oct handoff](../docs/design/handoff-2026-10-30-unified-list/Unified%20List%20-%2030%20Oct%202026.dc.html), as drawn: they are rows in Needs You, a system-scoped one survives the project filter, a closed one is a row in Finished, and each opens its card alone.
3. **Turning a repeated preference into a policy PR.** One action today; no authoring or preview flow is described.
4. **A standing "unattended stopped" indicator.** Answered by #980: both. A wax notice directly below the freshness banner on macOS and iOS, and a notice plus the status-item dot in the Mac menu bar, all driven by the sync snapshot's `unattended_operation` and never by the item list (`devlog/2026-10-02-1627-stopped-operation-indicator.md`).
5. **Snoozed items.** Deferred (◆2): a snoozed proposal leaves the list while snoozed; its return time needs a contract unit.
6. **Notification grouping and badges.** No grouping key or badge rule exists; push arrives in Phase 2.
7. **Device list and revocation.** Answered by #981: a Devices sheet on both platforms, opened from the toolbar, over a new `GET /devices` list (`devlog/2026-10-06-1442-device-list-and-revocation.md`).
8. **Per-run isolation class, credential mode, and egress profile.** Answered by #979: the run timeline carries each invocation's admitted facts (`admissions`), so the task page's Current Run reads its current run's latest execution admission for the run's role as its mono line (#2611), the run timeline shows each observed invocation's, and the execution-failure card shows the failed attempt's. A fact the admission did not record reads `Unavailable` (Current Run's one line skips it), never a value from current configuration.
9. **Daemon unreachable.** The external ntfy alert has no in-app counterpart beyond showing unreachability.
10. **Trust-profile review at onboarding.** Answered by plan revision 41: CLI-only; the app never shows it.
11. **Comprehension-defect capture** for sampled decision audits (plan §8, §9). The daemon records a defect via `freesided comprehension record-defect`; finding them stays manual (a sampled decision-audit workflow is a non-goal), and there is no in-app capture surface.
12. **Fault-class capture placement.** On the card at resolution, or a follow-up prompt?
13. **Inline or popover confirmation on macOS.** The consequential confirmation is a sheet on both platforms. An inline or popover confirmation would be lighter on macOS but needs a ruling on whether it still satisfies the explicit-destructive-confirmation rule (`devlog/2026-09-12-0945-chrome-under-design-language.md`).

Search and export of items or evidence are not in the plan at all; they
are named here only so nobody assumes they are.
